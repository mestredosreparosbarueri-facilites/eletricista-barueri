# Text Hook Templates

Use when writing on-screen text, first-frame text, covers, or any visual hook line.

## Keyword Dominance

Treat on-screen text like a YouTube thumbnail title. It has one job: make the right viewer stop.

The core move is keyword dominance. Use words the target audience already recognizes as their pain or their world. Recognition is faster than persuasion.

Where to find the keywords:

- high-view YouTube titles in the niche
- X posts and headlines that get clicks in the niche
- TikTok and Reel covers with high views
- the words the audience uses to complain, not the words experts use to explain

The keyword is the word that makes the viewer think "this is my world."

## Keyword Reference Table

Starter table. Replace with the user's niche language during `init`.

| Niche | Pain and slang words | Desire and outcome words |
|---|---|---|
| Productivity | slump, doomscroll, bedrot, brainrot, burnout | automation, flow state, leverage |

## Ten Fill-In Templates

1. `Here's one tip to achieve [desired outcome]`
2. `Come behind the scenes doing [specific task]`
3. `Why doing [common thing] will not help [desire]`
4. `[N] toxic mistakes to avoid if you want [desire]`
5. `You've been doing [specific topic] wrong your entire life`
6. `[N] things I wish I knew when I started [what you're doing]`
7. `How to go from [A] to [B]`
8. `When you finally do [recommended thing] and this is what happens`
9. `If I started [your profession] from scratch right now, here's what I'd do`
10. `[N] things that will help you [desired result]`

## How To Use The Templates

1. Pick the template that matches the content's payoff shape.
2. Fill the blanks with keywords from the niche, not abstract language.
3. Pass the result through the Three Rules in `hook-frameworks.md`: no attack, desire-led, payoff visible.
4. Tighten to thumbnail length: usually 6 to 14 words.
5. Check it against the spoken hook. If both say the same thing, change one. See Two-Pronged Hooks in `hook-frameworks.md`.

Weak fill:

```text
5 toxic mistakes to avoid if you want success
```

Strong fill:

```text
5 mistakes that keep your Reels stuck at 200 views
```

The difference is the keywords. "Success" belongs to no one. "Stuck at 200 views" belongs to one specific viewer.
