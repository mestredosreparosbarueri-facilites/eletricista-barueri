# Identity Command

Use when the user wants identity-based hooks, viewer callouts, educational openings, authority openings, or belief-shift Reels.

## Identity Stack Rules

- Keep the opening about the viewer.
- Use 4-6 viewer lines before introducing the creator.
- Widen once with "or even" if the audience has adjacent stages.
- Name the viewer's pain without attacking them.
- Name the mechanism, but do not explain it in the hook. Repeat the label once so it feels like the key.
- End with an open loop.
- Bring creator proof only after the stack.

## Annotated Example

This is the bar. Match this depth of specificity and this slow drag.

```text
If you've been building your little AI agency for a few months now...

watched all the videos, joined the communities, switched your offer like four times...

and you still haven't closed a single client...

or even if you closed one or two, and then it just... stopped.
and now you're sitting there stuck...

and you keep telling yourself it's the next tool. the next niche. the next funnel.

but if you're honest, you've been busy for months and you haven't actually moved.
```

Why it works, mapped to the rules:

- Each line adds a new concrete detail of the viewer's exact situation: "a few months now", "watched all the videos", "switched your offer like four times", "haven't closed a single client". None of them are generic.
- The "or even" widen catches the viewer one step ahead. "Or even if you closed one or two, and then it just... stopped." The viewer with one client was about to scroll because "haven't closed a single client" was not them. This line pulls them back.
- The enemy is the viewer's own coping loop, named without blame: "you keep telling yourself it's the next tool. the next niche. the next funnel." No guru, no "I figured this out."
- The close opens the loop as a quiet gut-punch: "you've been busy for months and you haven't actually moved." The viewer now needs to know what to do instead, and the creator has not appeared once.
- Zero "I", "me", or "my" in the entire stack. The creator enters only after this, at the fix.
- The trailing pauses force the slow drag that makes the accumulation land. The language stays small and spoken: "your little AI agency", "sitting there stuck".

## Output

Return 3 identity-stack options.

```markdown
## Option [N]: [viewer angle]

Spoken identity stack:
[4-8 spoken lines]

On-screen text:
[short title]

Mechanism named:
[mechanism]

Open loop:
[what remains unresolved]

Proof beat:
[where creator proof can enter, or "none needed"]

Rehook:
[line or visual reset]
```

## Quality Check

Reject identity stacks that:

- say "I" before the viewer is fully named
- use generic lines like "if you want to grow"
- blame the viewer
- explain the whole mechanism too early
- skip the "or even" widen when the audience has adjacent stages
- become a list of tips instead of a hook

## When Not To Use

- Pure story-time or shock-confession Reels, where a single hard line is stronger.
- Very short Reels (under 15 seconds), where the stack eats the runtime and leaves no room for payoff.
