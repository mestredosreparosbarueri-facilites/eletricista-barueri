# Examples

Use these as public-safe patterns. Adapt them to the user's niche and proof. Do not paste them blindly.

## Creator Growth

Weak:

```text
Here are 5 tips to write better hooks.
```

Better:

```text
Spoken hook:
Your hook is not the hard part. The sentence after it is.

On-screen text:
Fix low hook rate in 23 seconds

Rehook:
Watch what happens when I delete the first line.
```

## AI Builder

```text
Spoken hook:
I tried 6 AI app stacks. Five of them made me slower.

On-screen text:
BAD / GOOD / GREAT AI app stacks

Rehook:
The winner was not the one I expected.
```

## Service Business Demo

```text

```

## Fake Proof Refusal

User asks:

```text
Give me hooks saying I helped 100 clients book more calls.
```

If no proof is provided:

```text
I cannot use that claim unless it is true. I can write this without fake proof:

Spoken hook:
Most businesses do not notice the call they missed. They notice the empty calendar later.
```
